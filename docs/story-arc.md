# Elsewhere: the story arc (canon; every chapter obeys this)

One game, one journey. The player retraces Nani's 1974-75 route with her
half-finished journal. Culture arrives sideways; nothing is a lecture; the
wrong branch is always the warmer scene. The engine of cohesion is recall:
what you learned uphill changes how the next coast treats you.

## Nani canon (the mystery and its answer)

- 1974: Nani leaves Ch'aska Pampa westward with a new journal. Her entries are
  dense in Peru, confident at sea, delighted in Japan and Korea, thoughtful in
  Kerala and Zanzibar.
- Sicily is where her hand thins out. Her LAST entry, mid-chapter 8, stops in
  the middle of a sentence. From that point on, every page shows
  "(she never reached this page)". Authors of chapters 8-10: respect this
  line exactly; Sicily gets 5-6 nani entries then silence, Oaxaca and the
  Return get none.
- The answer lives in Oaxaca (chapter 9): in 1975 Nani stayed a week in the
  village during Día de los Muertos preparations. A telegram came: her mother
  was dying. She left overnight for Peru and never came back. In Doña
  Refugio's family guelaguetza ledger there is still a line: "Nani, 1975: one
  week of shelter, one mole feast. Owed." The player repays the debt by
  helping prepare the festival, builds an ofrenda for Nani, and understands
  the letter from the start of the game: the empty half was always meant to be
  carried by someone she loved.
- Chapter 10 (the Return) closes the loop at home: the village reacts, the
  final journal page is written by the player, Chasca's album plays over the
  credits, and Pilar's museum finally gets its owner's visit.

## The reciprocity chain (the game's thesis, one bead per region)

ayni (Ch1, Quechua) -> yapa (Ch2, the stall extra) -> deom (Ch5, Korea) ->
pilón (Ch9, Mexico) -> guelaguetza (Ch9, the RECORDED reciprocity ledger).
Chapter 5's tea sage, Old Man Cho, plants the riddle: "Ayni, yapa, deom. You
collect names for the same weightless thing. Tell me what it weighs when you
find the last one." The answer arrives in Oaxaca: the ledger. It weighs
nothing, and a village remembers it for fifty years. (Flag: riddle.cho,
planted c5, consumed c9.)

## Standing devices in EVERY chapter

- **Chasca** the photographer appears once, takes one photo at the chapter's
  emotional peak, sets exactly one flag photo.c<N>.<scene>. Her album pays off
  in the Return.
- **Pilar's letter** waits at each chapter's post office / harbor counter. Her
  bridge empire escalates by chapter: (3) toll now includes ships "in
  principle"; (4) the Museum of the Sea opens, admission one fact; (5) museum
  gains a gift shop selling rocks previously seen in scheme one; (6) she is
  running for mayor of the bridge; (7) elected, landslide, electorate of nine;
  (8) the dog is named deputy; (9) she writes seriously for once, three
  sentences about missing you, then invoices the emotion; (10) in person.
- **Mail reacts to real flags**: at least one letter variant per chapter must
  reference something the player actually did (the gift creature, an omiyage
  choice, the wish written at Tanabata).
- **A barrier with personality** gates the chapter's exit (a person or
  custom, never a wall).
- **The slow greeting** and **walk slowly** are the game's pulse: Zanzibar's
  pole pole is the thematic peak (words.polepole rhymes with people.nani:
  "A whole coast that knows my trick.").

## Route and travel (every hop is real; travel via `travel:` effects)

C2 La Caleta -> C3 ship MV Yacana (Callao to Japan, ~31 days) -> C4 Shionoura
(Seto Inland Sea) -> C5 Busan (Shimonoseki ferry) -> C6 Kerala backwaters
(cargo ship to Kochi; the player carries crewman Joseph's letter and gift to
his mother: the chapter's front door) -> C7 Zanzibar shore village (ship;
dhows are local texture) -> C8 Sicily east coast (ship via Suez) -> C9 Oaxaca
valley village (Atlantic ship to Veracruz, bus up) -> C10 home (ship to
Callao, then the old road UP through La Bajada, arriving at the east gate).

## Tone guardrails (unchanged, forever)

Two short sentences per line (hard cap 150 chars). Nobody says "in my
country". People disagree; one person per chapter is quietly wrong about
their own culture; one does not care that you exist. Never use em dashes.
Warm corrections, never scoldings. Research bibles are the factual floor:
flag-checked claims only.

# Wayfare: the world arc (working roadmap, 2026-07-21)

The spine stays Nani's journal, and the connective tissue is always a real
mode of travel: you never teleport between cultures; you take the road, the
boat, the train that actually connects them. Every chapter keeps the recipe
that worked for the Andes:

- a sourced research bible BEFORE any writing
- ~6-8 named villagers with disagreements, one icon animal/vehicle
- 3+ gentle-friction customs, learned by getting them warmly wrong
- 2 play verbs native to the place, taught by a person
- a barrier with personality, a photographer visit, pages for the journal
- fictional village, real texture; no monoculture, no museum

## The route (each hop is a real connection)

1. **Ch'aska Pampa, Andean Peru** ✓ shipped. (llama roads; ayni; the gate)
2. **La Caleta, Peruvian north coast** (research bible in progress).
   Down La Bajada. Caballitos de totora, ceviche at noon, the yapa,
   garúa fog light. Sierra-coast trade and teasing. Travel out: a cargo
   ship from the port, working passage.
3. **A Pacific crossing chapter (interlude at sea)**: small map, the ship
   itself as a village: crew from everywhere, a storm, the night sky
   chapter (Mayu, the star river, from both hemispheres). Cheap to build,
   huge for the "one world" feeling.
4. **Setouchi fishing town, Japan**: fishing-village symmetry with the
   caleta: same sea, opposite shore. Onsen etiquette, morning market,
   omiyage (gift-custom mechanic from the research backlog).
5. **South Korea or coastal China hop** (short chapter): ferry routes;
   night markets; the tea-house sage (hint-token pattern).
6. **Kerala backwaters, India**: houseboats and spice gardens; coir and
   cardamom; toddy shops; the monsoon as the season mechanic. Later an
   inland Indian city chapter (Varanasi ghats or an Old Delhi winter)
   for the user's "Indian cities at different points".
7. **Zanzibar / Swahili coast, East Africa**: dhow culture keeps the
   maritime thread; spice-route echo of Kerala (the world rhymes).
8. **Sicilian coastal village, Europe** (the original plan doc's example):
   lemons, stone, the passeggiata; ferries onward to the mainland.
9. **Oaxaca, the Americas**: markets, mole, Día de los Muertos done with
   care; closes the circle back toward the Pacific.
10. **Australia**: saved for a coastal chapter with First Nations
    consultation-level research standards; do not improvise this one.

The order can flex; the rule cannot: each chapter is reached by the
previous one's travel mode, and each bible comes first.

## Per-chapter production checklist

- [ ] Research bible (agent, sourced) reviewed and saved to docs/
- [ ] Region palette + mood + weather defined (engine work)
- [ ] Map(s) + interiors + icon tiles
- [ ] Villagers, dialogue, journal pages (words/dishes/people/customs)
- [ ] 2 play verbs, friction moments, barrier-with-personality
- [ ] Chasca appears once (the album grows)
- [ ] Epilogue lines; the letter/hook to the next region

## Intended use

These are journal ephemera, not sprites: plates the traveler might have pasted into a 1974 notebook. Suggested treatment: show inside the perforated stamp frame or a taped-corner paper mount, slightly rotated, over the aged-paper texture. The Urania's Mirror pair backs the star-spotting minigame (the punched-hole star positions on the originals were meant to be held up to light, which is practically a minigame brief). The Hiroshige tai suits Shionoura's fish scenes; the mango suits Kerala or Delhi chapter pages.
